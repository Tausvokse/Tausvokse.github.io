Privacy notice

Effective 17 November 2026. Product and privacy contact: tausvokseoff@gmail.com, Tausvokse, independent developer.

Website and checkout

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting providers process technical requests under their own policies. We do not add advertising analytics. Checkout is loaded only when you choose to buy; Paddle processes payment, tax and billing information under its privacy notice. Full payment-card details are not provided to UniCrates.

Orders and downloads

The delivery service processes an order reference, transaction reference, selected product and payment state to provide the purchased files. It stores a hash of the private download-access token; the browser keeps the token and order reference in session storage. You can save these in a purchase-access file to reopen downloads for 30 days from checkout preparation. Keep that file private. It does not contain payment-card details. The service also uses hashed network identifiers for request limits. Support correspondence may include the information you choose to send.

Pro activation

The activation service processes the signed license, license identifiers, installation public-key digests, hashed outgoing IP information and activation times. It uses these to enforce server allowances, prevent replay and manage revocation. It does not need your Minecraft world, player chat, payment-card information or the offline issuer private key. Cloudflare handles network requests and may process technical network data under its policies.

Retention and your choices

License and completed-order records are retained while needed to provide access, handle support, prevent fraud and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Short-lived request-rate and replay records expire automatically. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of product-support data; legally required transaction records and information needed to resolve disputes may need to be retained. Paddle handles requests concerning its payment records separately.

Lite does not require a license or contact the Pro activation service. A link to Pro appears once per player on each Lite server; the server stores that notice flag with player data. Your server operator controls its own player records.
